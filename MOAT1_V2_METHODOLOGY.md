# Moat 1 V2 methodology

V2 evidence does not change any financial score, rating, recommendation, valuation or DCF formula.

Evidence stages: 0 missing provenance; 1 contextual or policy language; 2 factual event grammar; 3 explicit annual event period supported by the financial history; 4 narrowly linked monetary amount; 5 matching structured financial row. Filing date and event period remain separate. Quarterly, year-to-date and partial-year sentences are not mapped onto annual evidence. A filing year alone does not establish the event year.

Amount extraction requires unambiguous linkage and explicit USD/U.S. dollars. Multiple amounts, conflicting years, ambiguous currency or uncertain linkage remain unavailable. Charges and losses retain their sign. Event families retain source evidence and never sum overlapping disclosures. Explicit named transactions are grouped when supported; otherwise source identity is retained. Conflicting family amounts remain unknown.

Recurrence describes observed disclosure history, not predictions. Explicit one-off language, multiple observed years of the same driver, isolated observations and unknown periods are distinguished. Generic net-income navigation topics do not establish recurrence.

Growth comparisons preserve negative growth, missing values, zero-denominator handling and N/M. Adjacent bridges compare revenue, operating income, pretax income, net income, OCF and FCF. A 10 percentage-point absolute growth gap is an evidence-review threshold, not a scoring change. Supported attribution means arithmetic reconciliation, not proven economic causation.

Pretax-to-net-income reconciliation uses reported tax expense. Operating-to-pretax uses the reported other-income subtotal without adding interest twice. Net-income-to-OCF uses the available cash-flow reconciliation and does not add working-capital detail to its subtotal. FCF requires OCF plus signed negative capital expenditure to equal reported FCF for both periods; changes distinguish CapEx increase, OCF weakness, mixed causes and incomplete evidence. No claim is made that CapEx is productive or growth investment.

Cash evidence retains OCF / Net Income and (Net Income - OCF) / Total Assets, preserving unavailable inputs. Tax paid and deferred tax are not treated as tax-related working capital; the latter remains explicitly unknown without a suitable field.

Core Revenue remains evidence-only, with unknown or caution states; the unimplemented legacy Core Revenue score stays Unavailable. Margin interpretation compares revenue/operating growth and observed margins. Historical ROIC uses the existing operating-income / (total debt + equity) definition on matching dates, not a new NOPAT or average-capital formula. Three adjacent annual observations support a trend; the stability interpretation uses one percentage point. Neither interpretation changes legacy scores.
