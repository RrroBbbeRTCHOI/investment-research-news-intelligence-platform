Weighted Positive Non-Core Contribution
{{ quality.weighted_positive_non_core }}
The model's weighted measure of identified positive non-core items divided by the absolute value of pretax income. It is not the total non-operating income share, not the total non-core earnings share, and not a complete accounting decomposition of core versus non-core earnings. Do not subtract this measure from 100% to infer a core earnings share. Negative items are tracked separately by the underlying model.
A zero value does not establish that no non-core items exist.
