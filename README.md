# Investment Research & News Intelligence Platform

A Python-based investment research platform designed to support fundamental analysis, earnings quality assessment, valuation, financial health analysis, SEC filing review, and market news intelligence.

The platform focuses on going beyond headline earnings by identifying factors such as non-operating gains that may affect the sustainability and quality of reported profits.

## Key Features

- **Fundamental Analysis** — Revenue growth, profitability, margins, ROE, ROIC, and financial trends
- **Earnings Quality** — Evaluates profit sustainability and flags non-operating earnings contributions
- **Valuation Analysis** — Historical and relative valuation comparisons
- **Financial Health** — Balance sheet and capital structure analysis
- **Stock Rating Model** — Multi-factor framework covering growth, profitability, earnings quality, valuation, and financial strength
- **SEC Filing Analysis** — Incorporates company filing data into the research workflow
- **News Intelligence** — Classifies market events and evaluates ticker relevance, urgency, and potential investment impact

## Technology

Python · pandas · NumPy · Flask · Financial APIs · SEC Filings · Data Analysis · News Intelligence
